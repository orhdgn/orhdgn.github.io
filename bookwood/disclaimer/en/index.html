Disclaimer

What we can promise, and what we can’t.

Bookwood helps you organize your library, read your books and keep track of loans. This page sets out its limits and what remains up to you.

Information in the app

Book details and covers

Titles, authors, covers and page counts come from open sources such as Open Library and may be incomplete or wrong. You can correct them with “Edit details” on the book’s page. Cover images belong to their publishers and rights holders; Bookwood shows them only to help you recognize your books.

Estimates

Book thickness, how many books fit on a shelf, arranging suggestions, missing volumes in a series and reading stats are worked out from the details available. They are approximate, and missing or wrong book details throw them off.

Loans and reminders

Loan records are only your own notes, not a contract or proof. Due-day reminders depend on your phone’s notification settings and may arrive late or not at all.

Your files and data

E-books

Bookwood doesn’t sell, download or share e-books. It only opens files you add yourself and doesn’t get around copy protection (DRM). Making sure you have the right to use those files is up to you.

Backups

Bookwood has no servers; your library lives on this device. Make a backup now and then in Settings › Backup so you can bring your library back if your phone is lost or broken. The backup password isn’t stored anywhere; if you forget it, the backup can’t be opened.

Other sites and brands

Bookstores and other sites

“Buy this book” and the app’s other links open other sites, which are responsible for their own content, prices and sales. Bookstore links marked “Ad” are affiliate links: if you shop through them, Bookwood may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
