Support

How can we help?

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours isn’t here, write to us.

Your library

How do I move my library to a new phone?

On your old phone, open Settings › Backup and tap “Back up now”. Save the file somewhere your new phone can reach, such as Google Drive, iCloud Drive or a computer. Then install Bookwood on the new phone, open the same place and tap “Restore from backup”. Backups work between iPhone and Android in both directions.

Can I use Bookwood on two devices?

Yes, but they don’t sync, because Bookwood has no account or server: each device keeps its own library. To copy your library to another device, make a backup and restore it there. Restoring replaces the library on that device.

Can I bring my books from another app?

Yes. Export your library from that app as a CSV file (it’s usually in the app’s settings or account page), then choose it in Settings › Import and export. Books already in your library are skipped.

Bookwood can’t find my book. What can I do?

Book details come from Open Library, which doesn’t have every book. Tap “Enter manually” and type the details yourself; the ISBN is filled in for you. You can also add the book to Open Library, so everyone who scans it later finds it.

Does Bookwood work offline?

Yes. Your shelves, loans, stats and e-books all work without a connection. Only looking up new books and showing ads need the internet; books you scan offline are added when you’re back online.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Bookwood stores is on your device. Books, shelves and libraries you delete wait in “Trash” for 30 days, and you can empty it at any time. Deleting the app removes everything from this device. Copies may remain in your phone’s own backup and in backup files you saved; delete those where you keep them.

Pro and purchases

How do I get Pro on my other devices?

Use the same App Store or Google Play account, open Settings › Bookwood Pro and tap “Restore purchases”. Pro moves between devices of the same store; a purchase made on the App Store can’t move to Google Play, or the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through the store, so refunds do too: on iPhone and iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, open your order history in Google Play. Each store decides under its own rules. If you get stuck, write to us.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Bookwood free for everyone, with every feature and no limit on books. They never appear while you read or add books. If you’d rather not see them, a one-time Bookwood Pro purchase removes them for good.
